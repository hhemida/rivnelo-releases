A little help.

Add a file

Open Add → Import audio or video. Choose a file in the system picker. Supported extensions: MP4, M4V, MOV, MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC; up to 500 MB each. Playback depends on codecs supported by your device.

Save a link

Use Add → Bookmark. HTTPS links open in their official app or browser. YouTube and Facebook are bookmarks, not downloadable sources.

Download approved media

Use a direct audio or video URL hosted at download.blender.org. Confirm the license allows your intended use. Keep the app open during transfer. Cancel from Library. If the app is terminated, restart the transfer; automatic background resume is not part of this preview.

Keep a separate copy

Use Share on iPhone, or Export file / Share on Android. Uninstalling removes the local library.

Updates

The app checks release metadata when you select Check for updates. New app versions will be delivered through the App Store or Google Play after publication. There is no executable-code update from this server.

Contact
